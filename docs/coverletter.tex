\newcommand{\ptitle}{Parallel Branch-and-Bound for Exact Pump Scheduling with Constant-Time Hydraulic Backtracking}
\newcommand{\mybodycontent}{
We are pleased to submit our manuscript, ``\ptitle,'' for consideration in \journal

Finding provably optimal pump schedules for real-world water distribution networks has long been intractable for exact methods. We address this directly with a distributed-memory Branch-and-Bound solver combining MPI-based parallel search, constant-time hydraulic state restoration via snapshot persistence, and cooperative global bound sharing. On a standard 24-hour benchmark, the solver achieves speedups of up to 115$\times$ over a sequential exact approach while preserving optimality guarantees --- and uncovers new best-known solutions that leading metaheuristics have not found.

Beyond the algorithmic contribution, this is fundamentally a paper about making rigorous optimisation practical for water system operation. Its hydraulic fidelity, EPANET integration, and direct relevance to day-ahead operational planning make it a natural fit for Water Research and its readership in water systems engineering and infrastructure management.

This manuscript is original, has not been submitted elsewhere, and all authors have approved the final version. We look forward to your consideration.
}
