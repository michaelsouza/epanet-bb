\newcommand{\ptitle}{Parallel Branch-and-Bound for Exact Pump Scheduling with Constant-Time Hydraulic Backtracking}
\newcommand{\highlights}{
\\
In this manuscript, we present a distributed-memory parallel Branch-and-Bound framework for exact pump scheduling in water distribution networks. The proposed approach integrates low-overhead hydraulic backtracking via state snapshots, cooperative pruning strategies to accelerate convergence, and deterministic load balancing for efficient distributed search. 
\\
Unlike metaheuristic methods that do not provide optimality guarantees, our solver delivers provably optimal 24-hour schedules and achieves speedups of up to 115$\times$ compared to sequential exact implementations. 
The method establishes new best-known solutions on a standard benchmark network under practical operational constraints.}
